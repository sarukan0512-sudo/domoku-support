FAQ

I bought a theme or Remove Ads, but it isn't showing

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy it.

Do purchases give any gameplay advantage?

No. Themes are purely cosmetic, and Remove Ads only removes interstitial ads. Optional reward ads for hints stay available if you want them.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple and Google. Request a refund through Apple or Google Play.

Delete your account and data

DomoKu creates an anonymous account so it can save your username and scores. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

  1. Email klipsodev@gmail.com with the subject "DomoKu account deletion".
  2. Include your DomoKu username so we can find your account.
  3. We'll confirm by email once your data has been deleted, within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your username and anonymous account ID
  • Your scores, stats and leaderboard entries

What isn't affected

  • Purchases are tied to your Apple or Google account, not your DomoKu account, so you can still restore them after deletion.
  • Data collected by advertising partners is governed by their own privacy policies.
